Location

Where to Find Us

Cafe Fontenebleu - Rua das panificadoras, 001 Sala 001 - Curitiba-PR
Telephone: (41) 3000-0001

We are in the old bakers' quarter, on the corner of a quiet street two blocks from the main square. The building is the one with the green awning; the entrance is on the side street, not on the avenue.

By Car

  • From the north - take the ring road to the Centro exit, then follow the signs to the historic quarter
  • From the south - leave the expressway at the second Centro exit and turn right after the cathedral
  • From the airport - approximately 25 minutes outside rush hour

Parking

Valet parking is available at the entrance from 19:00 every evening we are open. There is also a public garage on the same block, free for our guests for up to three hours with a ticket validated at the front desk.

Set in a unique, historic building, our private dining room is available to host your corporate events, parties, weddings, and more.

Public Transport

  • Metro - Praça Central station, a five-minute walk
  • Bus - lines 12, 40 and 108 stop at the corner of Rua das panificadoras
  • Taxi rank - on the main square, two blocks away

Before You Come

Opening hours, the reservation policy and our contact details are on the info page. If you are planning a private event, the special events page describes the room and the menus available.
