Our Story

Francesco and Jane Bienni opened Cafe Fontenebleu in 1996 with eight tables and a single wood oven. The restaurant grew slowly and deliberately, and today it occupies the whole of a historic building, with a main dining room, a private room and a cellar that holds more than 300 labels.

The kitchen is now led by their son Steven, a graduate of the Culinary Institute of America, who joined the family business after training in Italy. His arrival brought a modern accent to a traditional, Old World kitchen — a combination our guests have been generous about, as you can read on our review page.

Reservations

We recommend booking ahead for dinner and for any party of six or more. Reservations are held for 20 minutes past the booked time.

  • By telephone - (41) 3000-0001, during opening hours
  • By e-mail - reservas@cafefontenebleu.com.br
  • Parties of ten or more are served from a set menu, arranged in advance

Our special events manager, sommelier, and executive chef will work with you to make sure your special event is truly special.
