Cooking with Cafe Fontenebleu

After years of requests from our guests, chef Steven Bienni has recorded the recipes that made the restaurant's reputation. Cooking with Cafe Fontenebleu is a two-disc set filmed in our own kitchen, with no shortcuts and no studio tricks — the same dishes, prepared the same way we prepare them every evening.

Each recipe is shown from the market to the plate, including the preparations most cookbooks leave out: how to choose the fish, how to hold the knife, and what to do when the sauce breaks.

Disc One - Appetizers and Pasta

  1. Choosing produce at the market
  2. Roasted Tomato Soup with goat cheese croutons and basil puree
  3. Fondue of Brie, Goat Cheese and Gruyere
  4. Fresh pasta - mixing, resting and rolling by hand
  5. Penne con Pomodoro Fresco e Provolone
  6. Gnocchi al Forno con Salsiccia

Disc Two - Main Courses and Desserts

  1. Filleting and portioning whole fish
  2. Pancetta-wrapped Sea Scallops with tarragon puree
  3. Pollo Farcito con Fontina e Prosciutto
  4. Vitello Dolce Bacio
  5. Pumpkin Creme Brulee
  6. Sacher Torte - three layers, filled with house-made apricot jam

The utilization of regional seafood with the accompaniment of our fresh-grown herbs is something I'm avid about.

What's Included

  • Two discs, 240 minutes of instruction in total
  • A 48-page printed booklet with every recipe and quantity
  • Subtitles in Portuguese, English and Italian
  • A bonus chapter on pairing our cellar with each course
